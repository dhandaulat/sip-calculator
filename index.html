What is a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a smart and hassle-free mode for investing money in mutual funds. SIP allows you to invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals (monthly, quarterly, or weekly) rather than making a one-time lump-sum investment. It instills financial discipline and helps you build wealth over the long term without worrying about market fluctuations.

How Does a SIP Calculator Work?

The SIP Formula

The mathematical formula used to calculate SIP returns is as follows:

M = P × [ ({1 + i}n - 1) / i ] × (1 + i)

Where:

  • M: The future amount you will receive upon maturity.
  • P: The monthly amount you invest via SIP.
  • i: Periodic rate of return (Annual Rate divided by 12 and then by 100).
  • n: Total number of monthly installments (Years × 12).

Benefits of Investing via SIP

Investing in mutual funds through a SIP offers multiple compounding advantages over traditional saving methods:

  • Power of Compounding: When you invest regularly, your earned returns are reinvested, which generates further returns over time. The earlier you start, the bigger the corpus.
  • Rupee Cost Averaging: Since you invest a fixed amount every month, you automatically buy more mutual fund units when the market is low and fewer units when the market is high. This averages out the overall purchase cost.
  • No Need to Time the Market: Trying to predict stock market highs and lows is risky. SIP eliminates this guesswork completely, making it safer for retail investors.
  • Convenience and Flexibility: You can start with an amount as low as ₹500 per month. You can also pause, stop, or step up your investment whenever you want.
